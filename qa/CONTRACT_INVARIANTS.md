# Contract invariants

No duplicate settlement; unsettled obligations remain fully backed; released ≤ budget; only authorized participants/verifiers/arbitrator can transition; attestation domain/current sequence/rules/report match; consumed signatures cannot replay; submission history cannot be overwritten; revisions limited by funded policy/deadline; errors do not consume revision; disputes freeze ordinary settlement; cancelled/unaccepted Pact refund rules remain safe; V1 tests remain intact.
